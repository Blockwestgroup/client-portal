My NoCo favorite this week
FoCo Cafe | 225 Maple St, Fort Collins
A nonprofit, pay-what-you-can cafe where everyone gets a seat at the table. They serve fresh soups, salads and sandwiches made with local, seasonal ingredients, and if you can give a little extra, you can pay it forward for someone else. On Thanksgiving Day they're celebrating their 12th anniversary with a donation-based lunch from noon to 3, and everyone is welcome. Pre-order meals are $15, with a pickup window of 9 to 11 AM.

Seasonal home tip
When the house
fills up
A full house is the best kind of busy. Five little things keep the holiday about the people, not the plumber.
01Grease goes in the trash. Scrape pans and peels into the garbage, not down the drain or disposal.
02Find your water shutoff. Know where it is before you ever need it.
03Plunger in every guest bath. Your guests will quietly thank you.
04Ice melt by the door. Keep steps and walkways safe for late-night goodbyes.
05Nudge the thermostat down. A hot oven and a full house warm things up fast.
